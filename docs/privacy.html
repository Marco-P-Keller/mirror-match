Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Mirror Match is published by Connexa GmbH. We built it to collect as little data as possible: we do not collect, store or share any personal data.

What stays on your device

Your name (optional), scores, streaks, skins and settings are stored locally on your device only. We have no servers and no user accounts.

Links you share

When you choose to share a challenge, the link contains your display name, your score and the game seed (or the shape of a custom move). You decide whether and with whom to share it, using the system share sheet. We never see it.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment information. Subscription status is read from Apple on your device.

Notifications

If you enable the daily reminder, a local notification is scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere.

Tracking and analytics

Mirror Match contains no advertising, no third-party analytics SDKs and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Marco-P-Keller/mirror-match/issues.
