FAQ

How do friend challenges work?
Finish a challenge run, tap “Challenge a Friend” and send the link. When your friend taps it, Mirror Match opens with the same 5 moves and your score to beat. No account required.

Who are the “AI Ghosts”?
Computer-controlled opponents for solo duels. Their skill grows with your rank.

What are tickets?
Tickets are used for rematches, daily retries and streak repairs. You get 3 free tickets every day. Mirror Match Pro includes unlimited tickets.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings on your iPhone → your name → Subscriptions → Mirror Match Pro → Cancel. Or use Settings → Manage Subscription inside the app.

Restore purchases?
In the app: Settings → Restore Purchases.
