Can you copy this better than your friends in 3 seconds?

Mirror Match is the ultra-fast copy battle. Watch a move, then repeat it as precisely and as quickly as you can: swipe, tap, draw circles, zigzags, stars and hearts, or pinch with two fingers. A round takes about 10 seconds, so you can win your first match before your coffee gets cold.

HOW IT WORKS
1. WATCH: a glowing trail shows the move.
2. COPY: recreate it from memory.
3. WIN: the most accurate and fastest player takes the round. Win 3 rounds to win the match.

GAME MODES
• QUICK DUEL: best of 5 against an AI ghost. The ghosts get smarter as you climb from Rookie to Legend.
• DAILY CHALLENGE: the same 5 moves for every player, every day. Build a streak, beat your best score and share your result.
• CHALLENGE A FRIEND: play 5 moves, send a link, and your friend plays the exact same moves against your score. No account, no sign-up.
• FACE TO FACE: two players, one phone, split screen. Both of you copy the move at the same time.
• CUSTOM MOVES: draw your own move and send it to anyone. Can they copy it?
• REPLAY VIDEOS: every match becomes a ready-to-share 9:16 clip for TikTok, Reels, Stories and Shorts, rendered right on your phone.
